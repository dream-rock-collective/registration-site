About Dream-Rock Collective

It is only by working together can we create the world we want to live in. I believe in the power of creativity and imagination and collective power of everyday average people. Dream-Rock Collective began as a way to bring the community together and support those impacted by systemic violence. Merging the power of art with community reinvestment, Dream-Rock Collective focus’ on fun and engaged living in the world.

We are donating 50% of our profits to local mutual aids and nonprofits that can do the work in impacted and underinvested communities. Let’s see if connection and joy can fund programs that need our support!

50% of profits towards:

  • Local San Diego initiatives partnering with “We All We Got,” a San Diego mutual aid
  • Climate Justice partnering with Indigenous Climate Action
  • Helping people impacted by ICE partnering with Otay Mesa Detention Resistance

As a subscriber, you can determine where your dollars go, dividing up your 50% in equal parts, or whichever you would like to fund.

Financial transparency is a key value of Dream-Rock, and will be updated quarterly as the mail club grows.

About Me

Leeya Appleby was raised in Hawaii and has lived in San Diego, California for her entire adult life. She has a BA in Philosophy and BA in Psychology and a Master’s in Peace Studies and Social Justice. She loves to read and write about nature and is always learning more what it means to be alive in this particular time. Leeya is an oil painter, printmaker, and collage artist. Her subjects focus on romanticizing the magic of everyday life to art activism focused on climate justice and human rights. Leeya often creates art around the experience it feels being gay and neurodiverse—capturing the joy and complexities that come with being alive.
