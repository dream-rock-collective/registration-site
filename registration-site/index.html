About Me

Leeya Appleby lives in San Diego, California. She is a painter, printmaker, and collage artist. Her subjects range from whimsical, imaginative pieces to art activism to celebrating the magic in everyday life–capturing the joy and complexities that come with being alive.

Dream-Rock Collective began as an intersectional way to bring the community together and support those impacted by systemic violence. Merging the power of art with community reinvestment, Dream-Rock Collective reimagines business by giving half of our profits to our local community. Only by working together can we create the world we want to live in. We are donating 50% of our profits to local mutual aids and nonprofits that can do the work in impacted and underinvested communities.

50% of profits towards

  • local San Diego initiatives
  • Climate Justice
  • Helping people impacted by ICE
  • Racial Justice + Education
