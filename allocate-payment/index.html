Mutual aid is about solidarity, not charity

Indigenous Climate Action

Indigenous-led organization guided by knowledge keepers, water & land defenders from communities and regions across the country.

a) Strategic disruption of harmful colonial practices, policies, and processes and amplify Indigenous modalities of creative expression and storytelling;

b) Nurturing community members through understandings of healing justice, community engagement, and strategic climate education and training;

c) Exploring new climate solutions with community members at the grassroots, grasstops, and in elected leadership that center Indigenous values to imagine and create a just future.

Otay Mesa Detention Resistance

Organizing against the inhumane detentions and human rights abuses at Otay Mesa Detention Center.

The three areas of focus:

  • Supporting people in detention centers by funding phone calls, contacting family outside, transporting them, and organizing their post-release support
  • Supporting people once released
  • Advocacy, researching federal, state, and local policies, and trainings.
